Friction shifts with tenure

The main complaint at each stage of membership.

0–3 months
Expectations gap at onboarding

Unclear tours, confusing pool schedules, parking meter surprises and class cancellations on early visits.

3–12 months
Habits fade and booking frustrates

Attendance drops off, weekly online class registration adds friction and the eGym circuit gets crowded.

1+ years
Aging facilities wear on loyalty

Recurring pool and sauna downtime, instructor and schedule changes, plumbing issues and the Birmingham closure.

Patterns over time

Jan–Apr 2026

Birmingham closure spike

Cancellations surged after the Birmingham Family YMCA closed permanently. Members transferred to South Oakland or Farmington or left for private clubs.

Early 2026

Weekly registration backlash

Mandatory weekly online class registration drew complaints, with the heaviest tech friction among senior group exercise members.

Every Aug–Sep

Summer-to-fall churn

Lost interest and financial exits spike each year after youth camps end and outdoor pools close.

Data notes

  • The primary-reason chart sums to 100%. The detailed table sums to 80.2% and omits lost interest and alternative facilities.
  • Parking (17.4%) is labeled Boll-specific but appears alongside regional shares. Confirm which base it uses.
  • Boll reports only relocation and lost interest. The remaining 29.2% of Boll exits has no breakdown.
  • Medical (chart) and staffing (table) both show 7.2%. Confirm this is a coincidence and not a copy error.
  • The source lists the lowest-rated range as 3.1–5.8, but the lowest area shown is 4.2. One or more low-rated areas may be missing.
